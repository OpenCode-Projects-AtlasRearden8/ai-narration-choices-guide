Start Here: Choosing Your Audiobook Narration

The Audiobook Voice Decision Guide — by Asdale Dynamics, LLC

Who this guide is for

You wrote a book. You know an audiobook can reach ears a printed page never will. But a question is staring at you:

Should the voice on that audiobook be yours, a paid human narrator, or an AI voice?

This guide helps you answer that question with a clear head, a realistic budget, and a surprisingly small amount of homework. It is written in plain English, like a friend would explain it — never condescending, never full of jargon you don't need.

What this guide covers

Seven short chapters, four worksheets, one interactive decision tree:

ChapterWhat you'll learn
1. Human vs AI voiceThe honest trade-offs between hiring a narrator, ordering an AI voice, and reading it yourself
2. Platform rules in plain wordsHow Audible/ACX, Findaway/Spotify, Google Play, and others treat exclusivity, AI voices, and royalties
3. Sample-listening rubricA 5-dimension scorecard so your ears stop lying to you
4. Rights and disclosure basicsWho owns what, when you must disclose AI narration, and how to stay clean
5. Budget laddersFive spend levels, from $0 to premium, with what each one actually buys
6. Launch-day QC checklistThe once-over that saves you from a re-upload nightmare

Worksheets live in docs/worksheets/, and the interactive version is on the website.

What this guide is not

This guide assumes you are hiring out the narration or ordering an AI voice. If you want to record the narration yourself — your own voice, your own closet-studio, your own mic technique — that is a different skill tree, and it's covered by its own companion volume, Audiobook Recording Handbook. This guide doesn't repeat that material; the two are designed to sit side by side. Start with this guide to choose your voice strategy, and if the answer is "do it yourself," walk over to the handbook for the how-to of recording.

How to use it (15 minutes, honest)

  1. Read chapters 1 and 2. That's the actual decision. (~10 minutes)
  2. Fill Worksheet 1 (voice decision). (~5 minutes)
  3. If you're hiring a human or ordering an AI voice, skim chapters 3–5 before you spend money.
  4. Pin chapter 6 somewhere you can find it on publication day.

That's it. You will know more about audiobook narration licensing than you did before, and you'll have paper proof of how you decided.

Our bias, up front

This guide has no affiliate links, no preferred vendors, and no referral deals. When platforms are named, they're named because you'll meet them anyway. Prices and rules are described as of the date on each chapter; every platform changes terms occasionally, so always check the platform's own current page before signing anything. When we say "verify current terms," we mean it — that's not hedging, it's the whole job.
