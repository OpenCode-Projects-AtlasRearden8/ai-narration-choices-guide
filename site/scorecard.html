Listening Rubric Scorer

Score any narration sample — human or AI — on five dimensions (1–5 each), from Chapter 3. Listen twice on different days; your ears otherwise flatter or penalize at random.

1. Voice–book fit — does the voice belong in this book's world?
2. Pacing and rhythm — conducted, or just steady?
3. Character differentiation — who's speaking without the text saying so?
4. Technical polish — breaths, clicks, echo, clipping (earbuds + phone speaker)
5. Consistency across chapters — middle chapters checked, not just the opener

Any dimension at 2 or below is a red flag even at a passing total — walk-away territory described in Chapter 3. The printable log sheet is Worksheet 2.
