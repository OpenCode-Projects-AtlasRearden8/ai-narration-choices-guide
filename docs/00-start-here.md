# Start Here: Choosing Your Audiobook Narration

*The Audiobook Voice Decision Guide — by Asdale Dynamics, LLC*

---

## Who this guide is for

You wrote a book. You know an audiobook can reach ears a printed page never will. But a
question is staring at you:

**Should the voice on that audiobook be yours, a paid human narrator, or an AI voice?**

This guide helps you answer that question with a clear head, a realistic budget, and a
surprisingly small amount of homework. It is written in plain English, like a friend
would explain it — never condescending, never full of jargon you don't need.

## What this guide is *not*

This guide assumes you are **hiring out the narration or ordering an AI voice**. If you
want to record the narration yourself — your own voice, your own closet-studio, your own
mic technique — that is a different skill tree, and it's covered by its own companion
volume, **Audiobook Recording Handbook**. This guide doesn't repeat that material; the
two are designed to sit side by side. Start with *this* guide to choose your voice
strategy, and if the answer is "do it yourself," walk over to the handbook for the how-to
of recording.

## How to use it (15 minutes, honest)

1. **Read chapters 1 and 2.** That's the actual decision. (~10 minutes)
2. **Fill Worksheet 1** (voice decision). (~5 minutes)
3. If you're hiring a human or ordering an AI voice, **skim chapters 3–5** before you
   spend money.
4. **Pin chapter 6** somewhere you can find it on publication day.

That's it. You will know more about audiobook narration licensing than you did before,
and you'll have paper proof of how you decided.

## Our bias, up front

This guide has **no affiliate links, no preferred vendors, and no referral deals**. When
platforms are named, they're named because you'll meet them anyway. Prices and rules are
described as of the date on each chapter; every platform changes terms occasionally, so
always check the platform's own current page before signing anything. When we say
"verify current terms," we mean it — that's not hedging, it's the whole job.
