# Worksheet 4: Launch-Day QC Checklist

*From [Chapter 6](../06-launch-day-qc.md). Print one per launch. Interactive version on
the website. Do these in order; the first check finds most problems anyway.*

---

## Launch info

- Book title: ______________________________
- Audio edition upload date: ______________
- Platform(s): ______________________________
- Narrator / voice: ______________________________  AI narration? Y / N
- Exclusivity status: exclusive / non-exclusive / own-store

## A. Verify audio

- [ ] Retail sample plays correctly, no dead air before first word
- [ ] Sample sounds equally good on earbuds AND phone speaker
- [ ] Chapter transitions clean (last 30s of ch.3 → first 30s of ch.4)
- [ ] Opening credit "This is [title], narrated by [name or honest AI label]"
- [ ] Closing credit matches; no truncation into noise
- [ ] Catalog runtime matches the audio's true runtime
- [ ] Chapter metadata titles are human-sensible, not "track7final"
- [ ] Bitrate / format / channel specs match the platform's current published spec

## B. Verify metadata & rights

- [ ] Narrator credit spelled exactly as it should appear
- [ ] AI-narration disclosure present in my own description, even if platform also
      labels it
- [ ] Copyright year + holder consistent in audio metadata and upload form
- [ ] Sample chapter chosen for grip, not for "it's first"
- [ ] Title/series spelling matches ebook edition exactly
- [ ] Rights paperwork (contract or platform terms screenshot) filed where I control it

## C. Verify platform & pricing

- [ ] Price deliberate (not a panic discount); consistent with my budget rung
- [ ] Pre-order vs launch decided on purpose
- [ ] Exclusivity term matches my actual distribution plan
- [ ] Every intended store shows a LIVE listing, not "processing"

## D. The human test

- [ ] One non-author listener heard 10 minutes of the final retail sample on *their*
      device and answered "would you keep listening?"
- [ ] Their answer recorded: ______________________________
- [ ] Action decided if "no": fix sample / fix audio / accept and move on: ________

## E. Post-launch hygiene (first week)

- [ ] Audiobook linked on my website's book page
- [ ] Newsletter / reader arc updated with the new edition
- [ ] Listing screenshot saved (ASIN or equivalent, "narrated by" display)
- [ ] Actual production cost logged back into my budget planner
- [ ] First three reviews checked; notes taken for the next book's voice decision

## If something fails

- [ ] One corrected file, one calm resubmission note — *not* a barrage of re-uploads
- [ ] Date fixed & resubmitted: ______________  Confirmed live: ______________
