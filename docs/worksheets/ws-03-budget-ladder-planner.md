# Worksheet 3: Budget-Ladder Planner

*From [Chapter 5](../05-budget-ladders.md). Fill me in before you ask anyone for a
quote, so the quote lands on real numbers instead of vibes.*

---

## Step 1: Size the book

- Word count: ______________
- × 160 words per minute ÷ 60 = **estimated finished hours: __________** (use ~3.2 hr
  per 30k words as a check)

## Step 2: Pick a rung

| Rung | Cost shape | What you get | My number |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — DIY | $0 cash; hours + gear | Your voice, full control, longest calendar | $ ____ (gear) |
| 1 — AI voice | usually < $250, often < $50 | Clean but flat; best on nonfiction; disclose | $ ____ |
| 2 — Early-career human | $250–$1,000 per project | Competent narration; some risk on polish | $ ____ |
| 3 — Established human | ~$150–$350 PFH (book total ~$1,000–$2,500) | The classic pro audiobook; you own the audio | $ ____ |
| 4 — Experienced / union | $2,500+ (often $400+ PFH) | Performance as marketing; paper the deal | $ ____ |

- Target rung: ______
- Cost ceiling for the target: $ ______________ (finished hours × PFH rate, plus a
  10–15% buffer for revisions and re-records)

## Step 3: Reality check against sales

- Current ebook / print average monthly revenue: $ __________
- Break-even pace for 12-month payback (cost ÷ 12): $ __________ / month
- Is that plausible for this book's audience size? **Y / N**
- If **N**: drop one rung below your target and redo Step 3. Repeat honestly.
- Rung chosen after reality check: ______

## Step 4: Terms to hold firm on (from [Chapter 4](../04-rights-and-disclosure.md))

- [ ] Flat price and payment schedule in writing (suggest 50% / 50% on approval)
- [ ] Revision rounds included, and what counts as one
- [ ] Assignment of audio ownership to me on final payment
- [ ] Approval windows defined (e.g., 5 business days each side)
- [ ] If platform-mediated: exclusivity term, royalty rate, exit rules — screenshotted
      the day I clicked agree

## Step 5: Spend plan if I'm at Rung 1 today

- AI edition ships at a normal price (not an embarrassed discount).
- Description names the narration honestly ("AI-narrated edition" or platform's term).
- Sales from this edition fund Rung 3 on the *next* book. Next book target:
  Rung ____ by date ____________
